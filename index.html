National Parks Without a Car

63 U.S. national parks, scored on how easy they are to reach and explore using public transit

About This Project

This map scores 63 U.S. national parks on how realistic it is to visit them without renting or driving a car. Scores are a personal, transparent heuristic — not an official NPS rating.

Two separate scores, both out of 5

Getting somewhere without a car is really two different questions, so this map answers them separately instead of blending them into one number:

  • Getting To the Park (0-5): 🚌 Ground transit from the nearest airport/town to the park (0-2) + 🚆 Train/Amtrak access (0-1.5) + ✈ Nearest airport (0-1.5).
  • Getting Around the Park (up to 5): 🚌 In-park shuttle quality (0-2, scaled to 0-5) + 📅 Seasonality (-0.5 to +0.5, added unscaled — a great shuttle that only runs part of the year still loses a little credit).

By default, the map marker color reflects Getting To the park — since a great in-park shuttle doesn't help if you can't reach the park in the first place. Use the "Map colors" buttons to switch the marker color to Getting Around, or a Combined score (the average of both).

How airport and train scores are calculated

Both start at a maximum of 1.5 points and lose 0.01 point for every 2 miles of distance (0.005/mile) — reaching 0 at 300 miles away. A park with an airport or train station right at its entrance scores the full 1.5.

Color key (same thresholds for every mode, all on a 0-5 scale)

Green = 3-5 (easy). Yellow = 2-2.99 (moderate). Red = 0-1.99 (difficult).
