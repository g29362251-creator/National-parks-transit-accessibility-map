National Parks Without a Car

63 U.S. national parks, scored on how easy they are to reach and explore using public transit

About This Project

This map scores 63 U.S. national parks on how realistic it is to visit them without renting or driving a car. Scores are a personal, transparent heuristic — not an official NPS rating.

How the score works

Each park earns points across four categories, summed into a total (roughly -0.5 to 5.5, displayed as 0-5):

  • 🚌 Park Transit (0-2): How good the shuttle/transit system is once you're inside the park.
  • 🚂 Train Access (0-1.5): How close and direct the nearest Amtrak/train connection is.
  • 🚍 Ground Transit (0-1): Bus/shuttle options connecting the nearest airport or town to the park.
  • 📅 Seasonality (-0.5 to +0.5): Whether transit runs year-round or only a few months.

Color key

Green = 3 and above (easy without a car). Yellow = 2 to 2.99 (moderate, doable with planning). Red = below 2 (difficult, a car is recommended).
